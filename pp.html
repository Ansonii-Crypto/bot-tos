Catterix Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 31 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Catterix ("the Bot") collects, stores, and uses information when you interact with it through Discord.

1. Information We Collect

The Bot only stores information that is necessary to provide its features and functionality.

This may include:

  • Discord User IDs.
  • Discord Server (Guild) IDs.
  • Discord Channel IDs where configuration is required.
  • User preferences and custom settings.
  • Economy data, such as balances, items, or statistics.
  • Server configuration data, such as prefixes or enabled features.

2. Why We Collect This Information

Information is stored only to provide and maintain the Bot's features. For example:

  • User IDs allow the Bot to associate data with the correct account.
  • Guild IDs allow server-specific settings to be remembered.
  • Configuration data allows administrators to customise the Bot.
  • Economy data allows progression systems to function correctly.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

The Bot does not intentionally collect:

  • Passwords.
  • Private Discord messages unless explicitly required by a feature.
  • Payment information.
  • Personal information unrelated to Discord functionality.

4. Data Storage and Security

Stored data is protected using reasonable security measures. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

5. Data Retention and Removal

Data may be retained while it is needed to provide Bot functionality. Users or server administrators may request deletion of stored data.

Some data may be removed automatically when it is no longer required, or during maintenance and system updates.

6. Third-Party Services

The Bot operates through Discord and may be affected by Discord's own policies and services. We recommend reviewing Discord's Privacy Policy for information about how Discord handles your data.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Bot changes. Continued use of the Bot after updates means you accept the revised policy.
